Privacy Policy

FlashFit values user privacy and uses customer information only for shopping, delivery, support, and service improvement related to the platform.

1. Information we collect

We may collect your name, phone number, email address, delivery address, account details, order data, and payment-related information required to complete purchases.

2. How we use your information

  • To process and fulfill orders
  • To coordinate delivery
  • To provide customer support
  • To improve platform performance and customer experience

3. Payment security

FlashFit uses secure payment gateways and encrypted connections for online payment handling. Card details are not stored directly by the platform.

4. Sharing of information

Limited information may be shared with partner shopkeepers, delivery personnel, and payment providers only when required to complete an order or resolve a support request.

5. Data protection

Reasonable technical and operational safeguards are used to reduce unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure of customer information.

6. Cookies and preference storage

FlashFit may use cookies or local storage to improve performance, remember preferences, and support features such as saved pincode, cart state, and user experience personalization.

7. User rights

Customers may request account or data updates, and may contact support regarding account deletion or profile correction requests.

8. Policy updates

This policy may be revised as the platform evolves. Updated versions will be published on this page.
