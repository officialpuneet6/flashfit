Shipping & Delivery Policy

FlashFit ships only in active serviceable pincodes and aims for a 45-50 minute delivery window on eligible orders routed through nearby partner shops.

Active serviceable pincodes only Quick local dispatch model
Estimated timeline About 45-50 minutes for eligible local orders
Support email flashfithelp@gmail.com

1. Service areas

Delivery is available only in active serviceable pincodes supported by current operations and partner coverage.

2. Delivery timeline

  • Target delivery is around 45-50 minutes for eligible orders.
  • Actual timing may vary because of weather, traffic, shop load, festivals, or rider availability.
  • Delivery estimates begin after order acceptance and processing.

3. Charges

On the user side, delivery is shown as free. Final pricing is shown before payment confirmation.

4. Delivery attempts

  • Keep your registered mobile number reachable.
  • Incorrect address details may delay or cancel delivery.
  • Repeated failed deliveries may trigger temporary account restrictions.

5. Address accuracy

Customers are responsible for providing accurate house, street, city, state, and pincode information. Delivery issues caused by incorrect details may not be covered.

6. Partial or failed delivery

If an item becomes unavailable after order confirmation, FlashFit may coordinate partial delivery, substitution, or cancellation depending on the situation.

7. Wrong or damaged item

Report the issue quickly with photos and order information so support can review replacement, return, or refund options under the applicable policy.
